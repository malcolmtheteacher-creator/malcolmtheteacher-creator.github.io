Privacy

The short version: this site counts visits, keeps your progress in your own browser, and shows no adverts.

What is counted

When you open a page, the site records that a visit happened. It uses Google Analytics to do this, which places a small cookie in your browser.

  • Which pages are opened, and how people arrived at them.
  • The kind of device and browser, and roughly where in the world the visit came from.

It does not record your name or your email address. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for adverts. The numbers are only used to see which lessons are useful.

What stays with you

My Words and My Room remember the lessons you have opened and the words you are learning. That is saved in your own browser on your own device. It is not sent anywhere.

Switch counting off

You can stop your visits being counted. The choice is remembered in this browser.
