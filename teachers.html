Questions

May I copy the pages?

Yes. The pack is for one teacher, and you may copy the student pages for your own students and classes, in person or online. Please do not pass the pack itself on, post it publicly, or sell it.

How do I get it?

Each pack is a PDF. Payment and download are handled by Gumroad, and the file is yours to keep as soon as you pay. Your price may be shown in your own currency.

I teach online. Does it still help?

Yes. Every lesson also has a free interactive version on this site, made for screen-sharing. The address is on each teacher's page.

Does it work for one-to-one and for classes?

Both. Each plan is for 50 minutes, and the notes say what to cut when you are short of time.
